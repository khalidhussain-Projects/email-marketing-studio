# Claim timing check on tpllife.com

**Checked:** 2026-11-08
**Site last updated (per footer):** 2026-10-30
**Question:** Does tpllife.com say claims are paid in 24 hours?

## Answer

No. None of the pages we checked say this, and none give a claim payout time. Don't use "claims paid in 24 hours" or any other payout time in emails (brand voice rules 2 and 6).

## Pages checked

| Page | What it says about claim timing |
|---|---|
| https://tpllife.com/ | Nothing |
| https://tpllife.com/sehat-zindagi/ | Nothing. The only "hours" wording is "Accidental Emergency Expense Treatment (within 48 hours)". This is about when you get treatment, not when a claim is paid. |
| https://tpllife.com/sahulat/ | Nothing |
| https://tpllife.com/takaful/ | Nothing |
| https://tpllife.com/Doc/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Claim-Procedure.pdf | No payout time. See below. |

### Claim Procedure PDF (Group Family Takaful death claims)

The only time it gives, word for word:

> "In case the claim warrants an investigation, the company will complete the investigation as soon as possible but not later than 90 days of receipt of the last document."

It also says:

> "In order to validate the claim, TPL Life Insurance Limited reserve the right to ask for further requirements, if deemed necessary."

If we ever use the 90-day line, quote it exactly, include the line above with it, and send both to compliance.
