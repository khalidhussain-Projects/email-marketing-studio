## Differences from the website

- The website says "US$20,000 emergency coverage worldwide". The brochure says it is for emergencies **outside your area of cover**, up to 90 days per trip.
- The website says "Worldwide". The brochure excludes sanctioned countries.
- The website doesn't explain the Bronze asterisks. The brochure does.
- The brochure writes "Globewell - International Health Insurance Plan" with a hyphen. voice.md uses an en dash.
- The brochure gives a Karachi office at "19-B, S.M.C.H.S, Near Roomi Masjid, Shahrah-e-Faisal, Karachi." This is an old address. The current address is the one on tpllife.com: "20th Floor, Sky Tower – East Wing, Dolmen City, Block 4, Clifton, Karachi" (confirmed by the business, 2026-11-08). Use the website address.
