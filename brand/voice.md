# TPL Life Brand Voice

TPL Life Insurance Limited sells health and life insurance, both conventional and Takaful. Our emails go to everyone we serve: young people, families, salaried workers, business owners, older people, and above all our current policyholders and participants.

This is a regulated industry. Every email must follow SECP rules, and the compliance team must check every draft before it is sent.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Direct. Plain. Confident.**

- **Direct:** say the point in the first line. One idea per email.
- **Plain:** use short sentences and everyday words. If a reader has to stop and think about a word, change it.
- **Confident:** state our principles firmly, without hype. We don't beg, push or exaggerate.

## 2. Words we use

**For the people we write to**

- **Policyholder:** for conventional business.
- **Participant:** for Takaful business.
- Never mix the two in one email. If an email goes to both groups, write two versions.

**Everyday words**

- protect, protection, cover
- you and your family, your loved ones
- peace of mind
- simple, convenient, reliable, affordable
- Shariah-compliant (Takaful products only)

**Taglines**

- "Insurance Made Simple, Protection Made Powerful"
- "The most convenient insurance for you and your family."
- Takaful: "Ethical assurance for a secure future, the Shariah-compliant way."

**Product names (write them exactly like this)**

Conventional, health:

- Sehat Zindagi (Individual Health Insurance)
- Parental Insurance Plan
- Dental Insurance
- Women Specific Insurance Plan
- Globewell – International Health Insurance Plan

Conventional, app-based:

- TPL Sahulat
- Timeshield Insurance

Conventional, savings and life:

- Unit-Linked Savings Products: Grand Plan, Master Plan, Ultimate Plan, Zaryaab Plan
- Group Life (corporate)

Takaful:

- Individual Health Takaful
- Group Family Takaful

Product names come from tpllife.com (last updated 2026-10-30). Check them again before each campaign.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Pressure words:** "act now", "last chance", "don't miss out", "hurry", "limited time".
- **Fear:** no lines that use death, illness or accidents to scare the reader into buying.
- **Jargon:** "actuarial", "surrender value", "rider", "underwriting", "premium allocation". If we must use one, explain it in plain words in the same sentence.
- **Slang and emojis.**
- **Exclamation marks:** at most one per email, and best none.
- **"Customer":** use "policyholder" or "participant" instead.

## 4. Claims we must never make

1. **No guaranteed returns** on unit-linked or savings plans. Returns depend on how the investments perform. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
2. **No promises about claims.** Never say "every claim is paid", and never promise a payout time we can't back up.
3. **Never call a conventional product Shariah-compliant.** Only Takaful products are Shariah-compliant.
4. **No comparisons with named competitors.**
5. **Follow SECP rules.** Include the disclaimers and risk warnings each product needs, and get compliance sign-off before anything is sent.

**Claims already on tpllife.com come first.** If tpllife.com makes a claim, we may use it, even if a rule above would otherwise block it. When we do:

- Use the website's wording exactly. Don't make it stronger.
- Copy the disclaimers and risk warnings that go with it on the website.
- Note the page link and the date we checked it in the draft, so compliance can see where the claim came from.
- Compliance still signs off before anything is sent.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email is signed by the reader's Relationship Manager.

```
Stay protected,

Your Relationship Manager
TPL Life Insurance Limited
UAN: (021) 111 000 330 | tpllife.com
```

- The closing line is always "Stay protected,".
- Don't put a Relationship Manager's name in the sign-off.
- Use the UAN as the helpline number, written exactly as above.
