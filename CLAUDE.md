# Email Marketing Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for TPL Life Insurance Limited. It is for TPL Life only. If a request is for another brand, say so and ask before writing anything, because the rules below are written for TPL Life.

## Folders

- `brand/`: the business's voice, tone, colours, logos and key messages.
- `leads/`: notes about who we're writing to, such as audience groups and personas.
- `campaigns/`: one folder per campaign, holding its plan and email drafts.
- `notes/`: research, decisions and anything else worth keeping.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words, and avoid jargon.
- Never send email to real people. Draft emails only. Don't run any command or tool that sends email.
- Ask me before deleting anything, including files, folders and parts of a draft.
