# The Steep Club: Waitlist Launch Plan

- **Status:** Draft, for review
- **Written:** 2026-11-08
- **Campaign dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
- **Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.
- **Brand voice:** `brand/voice.md` (TPL Life), used at the owner's request. See "Brand voice notes" at the end.

Anything marked **[TBC]** is a fact we don't have yet. Fill it in before drafting emails.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** "Your First Steep"
- **Summary:** Turn our 2,000 waitlist members into paying Steep Club subscribers with a short, calm series of emails, one for each reader group.
- **Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a paid subscription between 7 December 2026 and 3 January 2027. That is 15% of the list.
- **Other goals:**
  - Learn which reader group converts best, to guide the next campaign.
  - Get at least 30 gift subscriptions.
  - Keep unsubscribes under 2% of the list.

**Is 300 realistic?** It is a stretch. Warm waitlists often convert at 5% to 15%. Hitting 300 needs a clear founding offer and good timing for gift buyers. See the risks in section 9.

## 2. Audience

All 2,000 people asked to hear from us, so they already know the brand. They are at the **decision** stage. The job is to remove doubts and make starting easy.

| Group | Est. size | Who they are | What they want | What holds them back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tea-curious beginners | [TBC] | New to loose-leaf tea. Like the idea, unsure where to start. | Guidance. Simple steps. Low risk. | "I won't know how to brew it." "What if I don't like it?" |
| Gift buyers | [TBC] | Want a thoughtful present for someone else. | An easy, good-looking gift that arrives on time. | Delivery dates. "Will they like it?" "Can I pick the length?" |
| Daily tea drinkers | [TBC] | Drink tea every day. Know what they like. | Better tea, variety and good value. | Price per cup. Whether they can choose or skip boxes. |

If we don't know which group each person is in, the first email asks them to choose (see Email 1).

**Profile, in one line:** "A tea lover on our waitlist who is ready to try, but wants to know it's simple, worth the price and easy to stop."

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** The Steep Club is open. Good tea arrives at your door each month, and it's simple to start, change or stop.

| Message | Best for | Proof we need |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple to start.** Each box comes with clear brewing steps. | Beginners | A brewing card or guide [TBC] |
| **A gift that keeps arriving.** Choose the length, add a note, and we send it. | Gift buyers | Gift options and delivery cut-off dates [TBC] |
| **Better tea, every day.** Curated teas at a fair price per cup. | Daily drinkers | Price per box and cups per box [TBC] |
| **You stay in control.** Skip, pause or cancel any time. | Everyone | The actual cancel and skip policy [TBC] |

**Message order in every email:** why it matters → what the club is → why us → one clear next step.

**Things we won't say:**
- No health claims, such as "detox", "boosts immunity" or "helps you sleep". We can't back them up.
- No pressure lines, such as "last chance", "hurry", "limited time" or "don't miss out". Founding-member offers are stated calmly, with the plain end date.
- No more than one exclamation mark per email (best none). No emojis or slang.

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **We fall short of 300.** 15% is high for one month. | Check at the halfway mark. If sign-ups are low, resend E4 with a new subject line to people who didn't open it. Also agree a fallback target, such as 200. |
| **Gift buyers miss the delivery dates.** | Put the real cut-off date in E3 and on the sign-up page. Stop gift-delivery promises after the cut-off. |
| **People ignore the "Which are you?" question in E1.** | Send non-clickers the general version of E2. Use what they click later to tag them. |
| **The holiday slump in late December.** | Keep 22 to 27 December email-free. Put the final reminder on 28 December. |

## Brand voice notes

The owner asked us to use `brand/voice.md`. It is written for TPL Life Insurance, so some rules don't fit a tea club. Here is how this plan handles them. The owner should confirm or change these before drafting starts.

| Rule in voice.md | How this plan uses it |
|---|---|
| Direct, plain, confident. One idea per email. | **Used as written.** |
| No pressure words, no fear, at most one exclamation mark, no emojis or slang | **Used as written.** |
| Use "policyholder" or "participant", never "customer" | Kept "never customer". Readers are called "members" or "you", because they hold no insurance policy. **Please confirm.** |
| Sign-off: "Stay protected, Your Relationship Manager, TPL Life Insurance Limited, UAN (021) 111 000 330" | **Not used yet. Decision needed.** It would send tea buyers to an insurance helpline and put TPL Life's name on another brand's email. We suggest a Steep Club sign-off instead. |
| SECP rules and compliance sign-off | Doesn't apply to tea. The owner's approval replaces it. |
| Insurance taglines and product names | Not used. |

**Our suggestion:** write a short `brand/steep-club-voice.md` that keeps the plain-English rules above and adds a Steep Club sign-off and a "no health claims" rule.
